Typography for the I Love Foobar collage

Compagnon by Juliette Duhé, Léa Pradine, Valentin Papon, Chloé Lozano,
and Sébastien Riollier. Distributed by Velvetyne.
https://velvetyne.fr/fonts/compagnon/
See Compagnon-LICENSE.txt.

FoobarMono-Medium.woff2 is a subset of JuliaMono Medium by cormullion
(https://juliamono.netlify.app/), trimmed to the characters this page needs
and renamed, as the SIL Open Font License requires for modified versions,
since "JuliaMono" is a Reserved Font Name. Licensed under the same OFL; see
JuliaMono-LICENSE.txt.

Piazzolla, Young Serif, and Commissioner are served by Google Fonts.
https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Piazzolla
https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Young+Serif
https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Commissioner

Image and text provenance, full source URLs, and supplied image credits are
preserved in docs/foobar-picks.json. Visible source labels are abbreviated
in the collage; images remain available at their original resolution.
